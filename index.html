Tic Tac Toe Game.

Welcome to the Tic Tac Toe Game. The rules for this game are simple: Choose either the X or O as your symbol. Once you have chosen your symbol, click the position in which you want to place your marker. The goal is to have three of your symbols in a row to win. THe orientation of these 'rows' may be diganoal or straight across, up or down across the board.
